# Privacy notice: publication checklist

The local page /privacy-policy/ is a review draft, not a legal-compliance certification. Do not publish as effective until actual practices and legal review are complete.

Confirmed from current website source: mailto enquiries, illustrative local previews, Google Fonts requests, no embedded analytics or contact form in inspected source. Netlify is the known public deployment. Hosting settings have not been audited. The user replied “yes” twice to bundled provider questions; this does not establish any provider identity, overseas processing or training permission. gov@nitiaide.com is the existing contact address and proposed privacy channel.

Confirm with the business and qualified Indian privacy counsel:
- Actual website analytics/cookies, hosting logging, email providers, subprocessors and processing countries, including configuration outside this repository.
- External AI inference providers, prompts/log retention, any training use, contractual protections and each departmental deployment boundary.
- Enquiry, security-log, departmental and backup retention periods; deletion workflow and lawful holds.
- Named grievance contact, usable postal address, identity checks, request response and escalation procedures.
- Security controls, breach response ownership and any applicable child-data processing.
- Applicable Indian law and staged commencement at publication; avoid asserting all DPDP obligations are already effective.
- Reconcile the homepage's absolute sovereignty/training claims with confirmed operational facts.
- Approve effective date, remove draft notice and noindex only after approval; confirm the footer link and multi-page production build.
