The problem we solve

Government has the information.
Finding it takes time.

Policies, orders and data sit across multiple systems in different formats — officers spend hours arriving at one answer. Departments hold vast data, but finding the right record means manual searches across papers, legacy systems, and languages. NitiAIDE unifies it into one secure platform, so officers ask in plain language and get verified, source-backed answers in seconds, with data never leaving government control.

Security

Your data. Your control. Always sovereign.

Public data carries obligations that do not transfer to a vendor. The architecture reflects that, and these terms hold on every engagement.

100%Of data processed and stored in India
ZeroOf your data used to train external models
EveryAction logged for an audit-ready trail
