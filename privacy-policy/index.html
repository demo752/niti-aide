01

Who we are and what this covers

NITI AIDE Solutions Private Limited (“NITI AIDE”, “we”, “us”) provides AI and information services for public administration. Our business contact location is Hyderabad, Telangana, India. This notice explains the handling of personal information connected with our public website, business enquiries and related communications.

Government deployments can involve different purposes, records and responsibilities. A department’s service-specific privacy notice and its agreement with NITI AIDE describe those arrangements. This general notice does not replace a department’s citizen notice or authorise access to government records.

02

Information involved

The information involved depends on how you interact with us:

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, organisation, role, contact details and the information you choose to send when requesting a demonstration or support.
  • Website delivery: internet protocol (IP) address, requested pages, browser information, timestamps and security or error records may be processed by website infrastructure providers to deliver and protect the site.
  • Correspondence: messages, meeting arrangements and records needed to follow up on a request or manage a business relationship.
  • Departmental services: depending on the authorised deployment, records, user identifiers, questions, conversations, source references and audit events may be processed to deliver that service.

Please do not send identity documents, confidential government records, health information, passwords or other sensitive material through a general demonstration enquiry. Arrange an approved transfer channel with the relevant department first.

03

Why information is used

Website and enquiry information is used to respond to questions, arrange demonstrations, provide requested assistance, administer business relationships, maintain website availability and investigate security incidents. Records may also be needed to meet applicable legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

The applicable legal justification depends on the activity and the law in force. Where consent is required, it must be obtained for the stated purpose and may be withdrawn. Simply browsing this website is not blanket consent to unrelated marketing or to the use of departmental records.

04

Government records and AI processing

For a departmental deployment, the responsible department determines the public-service purpose and authorised access. Where NITI AIDE acts on its behalf, the permitted processing, hosting, access controls, retention and assistance with individual requests must be specified in the deployment agreement and related notice.

Using records to retrieve information and prepare an answer is distinct from training an AI model. Whether a deployment uses external model providers, where inference occurs, what is retained and whether any model training is permitted must be expressly documented for that deployment. This website notice does not grant permission to train models on government or citizen data.

The public website’s conversation and dashboard previews contain illustrative examples. They are not a live departmental service, and selecting a preview language does not submit a citizen application. Questions about an actual public-service record or decision should be directed to the department responsible for it.

05

Service providers and disclosures

Hosting, email and other operational providers may process information needed to deliver the relevant service. The actual provider inventory, access permissions and contractual safeguards should be read with any deployment-specific notice. Connected messaging platforms or websites also have their own privacy notices.

Information may need to be disclosed to competent authorities where lawfully required, or to professional advisers for a relevant legal or business matter. Any disclosure must have an applicable legal basis and be limited to its purpose. A government logo shown on this website does not authorise information sharing with that organisation.

06

Website technologies and external links

The website build reviewed for this draft contains no first-party analytics script, advertising pixel, account login or contact-submission form. Its demonstration buttons open your email application; information is sent only when you send the message. Hosting-level analytics and any integrations added outside the source code still require confirmation before this notice is published.

Fonts are requested from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve them, which can disclose connection information such as your IP address and browser details. See Google Fonts’ privacy explanation. External links, including LinkedIn, take you to services governed by their own notices.

You can manage cookies and site storage in your browser. Before optional analytics or marketing technologies are enabled, their purposes, providers and required consent controls must be documented. This draft does not assert that an unverified tracking system is absent from the hosting environment.

07

Storage and processing locations

The public marketing website and business email are separate from a department’s production environment. Website hosting or external font delivery must not be interpreted as a promise that all website visitor information remains in India.

For government deployments, the approved hosting location, remote access arrangements, subprocessors and any cross-border processing must be specified in the deployment documentation and comply with applicable restrictions. Contact us for the arrangements applicable to a particular engagement.

08

Retention and deletion

Retention should be limited to the period needed for the stated purpose and any applicable legal requirement. Relevant considerations include whether an enquiry remains open, an ongoing contract, audit obligations, security investigations and legal claims.

Departmental records follow the department’s authorised retention and disposal requirements. Removing information from an active service may not immediately remove restricted backups or records subject to a lawful hold. The actual retention periods and backup-deletion schedule require confirmation before this draft becomes an effective notice.

09

Security and incident handling

Security arrangements must be appropriate to the information and deployment. They should address authorised access, confidentiality, protection in transit and at rest where applicable, monitoring and incident response. No website or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Suspected exposure involving NITI AIDE can be reported to gov@nitiaide.com. Include enough context to investigate without sending the exposed records themselves. Notification to departments, affected individuals or authorities will be governed by the applicable law and deployment responsibilities.

10

Your requests and choices

You can contact us to ask about the personal information relating to you, request correction or deletion, raise a privacy grievance, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. The rights available, identity checks, exceptions and response periods depend on the applicable law and its commencement provisions.

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and associated rules provide for rights including access to information, correction and erasure, grievance redressal and nomination, subject to their applicability and commencement. This notice does not limit any statutory right or create an exemption from an applicable obligation.

For records managed by a government department, contact that department first. NITI AIDE cannot independently change an official record or override a lawful retention requirement. We may need to route a request to the responsible organisation. Do not send an Aadhaar number or other identity document with your initial request; any necessary verification should use a proportionate, secure method.

11

Children’s information

This marketing website is directed to public-sector organisations and professional contacts. Public services may nevertheless involve children’s records. Any such processing requires a service-specific assessment, appropriate notices and applicable parental or guardian consent or lawful exemptions; it must not be assumed to be authorised by this website policy.

If you believe a child’s personal information has been sent to us through a general enquiry, contact the privacy address below so the circumstances can be reviewed.
